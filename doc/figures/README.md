## Drawing conventions

- The VDD symbol is `vin` (3.3 V); the GND symbol is `vss`. `vddd` (1.2 V) appears as the terminal `VDDD` in `ldo_ilim.svg`.
- Bulk terminals are not drawn. In the schematics every PMOS bulk is `vin` and every NMOS bulk is `vss`
  (this includes `Mcas`, whose source is `sense_d` but whose bulk is `vin`).
- Terminal labels name schematic nets: VREF=vref, VFB=vfb, VEOUT=eout, VOUT=vout, IBIAS=ibias (the gate
  line of the harness bias mirror), ENn=en_n, ILIMEN=ilim_en, VDDD=vddd, OC=oc, VTRIMk=vtrimk.
  A terminal label used twice in one figure is the same net.
- Amplifier polarity in `ldo_top.svg`, derived from `ldo_erramp.sch`: `vfb` is the non-inverting input
  (vfb up -> eout1 down -> eout up) and `vref` the inverting one; the pass PMOS inverts again, so the loop is negative.
- Device multipliers are written out where they matter: `Mpass` is one device with m=64.

## Simplifications (top level only)

- `x_amp` is drawn as an amplifier symbol (its transistors are in `ldo_erramp.svg`).
- The trim ladder below `vfb` (`Rbase`, `Rt0`-`Rt4`, `Msw0`-`Msw4`) is drawn as one resistor `R_trim`; `vtrim[4:0]` is not drawn. See `ldo_trim.svg`.
- In the current limiter, `Mocp`+`Mocn` are drawn as one inverter (they are a CMOS inverter between vin and vss);
  `Msd`, `Mdis_oc`, `Xinv_ie` and the OC flag path (`Roc`, `Moc`, `Xinv_oc`) are left out. All are in `ldo_ilim.svg`.
- Not drawn: `x_vref` (shown as the VREF terminal), `x_boost`, `x_en`, `x_pg`; and the `ibias`/`en_n` pins of `x_amp`.

## Verification

Each table below was generated by extracting connectivity from the `.sch` geometry (instance pin
positions from the PDK/local symbols, nets from the xschem wire labels) and comparing it, pin by pin,
with the connectivity of the figure's own wires (union of wires, junctions and pins). The extractor
reproduces `sim/netlist/*.spice` exactly for every LDO cell. A figure passes only when every drawn
pin lands on the schematic net, no figure net joins two schematic nets, no schematic net is split
(except supplies, which have one symbol per pin by rule), and every schematic device is either drawn or
listed as not drawn. The checker was confirmed to fail on a swapped mapping before use.

Render check (`tools/render.py`): every figure reports "all wire ends on pins or wires". No wire
crossings in any LDO figure.
